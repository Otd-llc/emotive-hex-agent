The stock assets, textures and the parts of the engine this build drops (the 2D engine, the moon, sun and eclipse geometry, the HDRI path, the bloom pass and the rest listed in the README) are not in `src/` or `dist/`, so their licenses do not apply here.

## Assets in `test/fixtures/agent/`

All 29 files (seven crystal shapes, the soul inclusion, the electric meshes in three sets, one detail map) are generated by One Thousand Drones' own Blender scripts with no third-party inputs, and are MIT like the code. Each file's generator, toolchain and `sha256` are recorded in [`test/fixtures/agent/PROVENANCE.json`](../test/fixtures/agent/PROVENANCE.json) and [`sha256.json`](../test/fixtures/agent/sha256.json); the source of truth is the host application's `public/agent/`.
